About Me

I'm a data scientist with a background in statistical modeling, predictive analysis, and hands-on work in Python and R. My experience spans applications in various fields (materials science, environmental data, financial data, etc), often through collaborative research projects.

I hold a PhD in Statistics and an MSc in Pure and Applied Mathematics, and I've had the chance to study and work both in Brazil and abroad, including as a visiting scholar at U.S. universities.

On this website you will find my project portfolio, as well as links to my blog and LinkedIn / GitHub profiles.
